Design approach

My workflow is intentional.

I start with the performance need, then design backwards from what learners should be able to do—not simply what they should know.

01 · DISCOVERAudience, context, performance gap
02 · DEFINEObjectives, scope, success criteria
03 · DESIGNStructure, activities, assessment
04 · DEVELOPStoryboard, SLM, eLearning build
05 · VALIDATESME review, QA, usability & alignment
